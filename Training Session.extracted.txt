are scattered through the text — we cannot standardize or reason 
about them until we know exactly where each concept sits.WHAT WE NEED
1Locate the span — the exact character range of every 
medical concept.
2Assign the type — CHẨN_ĐOÁN, TRIỆU_CHỨNG,

What is GLiNER?
A zero -shot span model. Given only the label names as a 
prompt, it finds and tags entities with no labeled training data - 
essential here, since the competition provides no training set.

❖Assertion Detection: The ProblemBaseline
37THE PROBLEM
“không khó thở” → NEGATED
“tiền sử tăng huyết áp” → HISTORICAL
“mẹ bị đái tháo đường” → FAMILY
In every row the span is correct, yet the clinical fact is the opposite 
of the surface words. Read literally, we would record active dyspnea, 
a current hypertension diagnosis, and the patient's own diabetes, 
none of which are true.WHAT WE NEED
1 Detect polarity: is the concept affirmed or negated?
2Detect temporality & experiencer: current vs. past, 
patient vs. family.
What is ConText?
A lightweight rule -based algorithm. For each concept it scans a 
few words around the span for trigger cues - e.g. “không ”, “tiền 
sử”, “mẹ” - and flips the assertion accordingly. Fast, 
transparent, and needs no training data.

❖NER EnhancementsNext Steps
45MOVES:  0.3 · text  + downstream NER precision feeds every stage after it, which is the highest -reach place to improve.
1 Fine -tune GLiNER on synthetic data
Turn the zero -shot model into a supervised one using the notes from 
the data -synthesis step, the single biggest precision & recall lever.2 Type -specific thresholds
Calibrate the confidence cut -off per concept type: symptoms over -
fire, tests under -fire. One global threshold leaves points on the table.
3 Smarter chunking + span -boundary refiner
Token -budget windows instead of character splits, plus a refiner that 
snaps predicted spans to exact character boundaries.4 Self-consistency / light ensemble
Combine multiple passes behind a precision guard, since the metric 
punishes over -emission hardest, so favour  agreement over raw recall.

privacy -gated. So we generate them, with labels attached by construction.
